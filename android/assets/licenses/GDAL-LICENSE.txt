GDAL licence, for the parts of BathyPicker's Android engine that follow GDAL's code

Proximity.java follows GDAL's gdalproximity.cpp, and the resampling and scale steps in
Builder.java follow GDAL's gdalwarpkernel.cpp and gdalwarpoperation.cpp (GDAL 3.13.3), so the
phone builds the same files as the desktop version's gdalwarp and gdal raster proximity.
